## 📖 Documentation Structure

Our documentation follows the [Diátaxis](https://diataxis.fr/) framework for clear, organized content:

### 🎯 [Tutorials](tutorials/)
*Learning-oriented • Acquisition of skills*

Step-by-step lessons to get you started. Perfect for newcomers who want to learn by doing.

### 🔧 [How-to Guides](how-to/)
*Problem-oriented • Application of skills*

Practical solutions to specific problems. For when you know what you want to achieve.

### 📚 [Reference](reference/)
*Information-oriented • Application of skills*  

Technical specifications, API documentation, and detailed information. For when you need precise details.

### 💡 [Explanation](explanation/)
*Understanding-oriented • Acquisition of skills*

Background concepts, design decisions, and the "why" behind our projects. For deeper understanding.
